Dog

The dog is a common domestic animal kept by humans as a pet, it is known for its loyalty.

Cat

The cat is another domestic animal and is known for catching mice.

Cow

The cow is a large farm animal, raised primarily for its milk, it feeds on grass

Duck

The duck is a common bird that lives near ponds and lakes, it has webbed feet.

Horse

The horse is a large domestic animal that is used for riding and is known for its speed.

Fish

The fish is an animal that only lives in water and breathes with its gills.
